# Product naming for Vietnamese users (worker namevn, 2026-11-16)

Round 2 after docs/naming.md. A plain list to choose from. No ranking, no pick. Not legal advice.

## 1. Criteria
- 2 or 3 syllables, 4 to 8 letters, read aloud correctly by a Vietnamese reader with no English spelling rules.
- Only natural Vietnamese sounds: no str/spr/ct clusters; ends in a vowel or n, m, ng, nh, c, ch, p, t.
- Works without tone marks; every Vietnamese-looking syllable checked with and without tones for rude or comic words and read-aloud homophones.
- Memorable by rhythm, vowel repeat or a concrete image. Families: a coined, b Vietnamese or Han-Viet words, c short international words, d blends.
- Neutral: no stealth, ghost, shadow, phantom, mask, bot, scraper register; not tied to Windows. International meaning check is from my own knowledge only.
- Pool: 100 own raw candidates cut to 40 finalists, plus names from the chatbots that passed the checklist. Table below has 46 names.
