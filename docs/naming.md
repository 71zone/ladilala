# Product naming (worker namescout, 2026-11-16)

Working name "WinMacro" was not final (history: the product is now named Ladilala). This is a screen, not legal advice.

## 1. Criteria
- 4 to 9 letters, easy to say and spell, no hyphens, usable as name.exe and as a folder name.
- Neutral: no stealth, bot, scraper, mask, ghost, shadow, phantom, anti-detect register; not tied to Windows.
- No rude or odd meaning in vi, es, pt, id, th, zh, ru, de, fr, ja (checked by my own knowledge only, see section 6).
- No known software product with the same name; prefer free .com or at least free .app/.dev.
- 60 raw candidates in 6 families were generated and cut. Real words (halyard, plinth, tessera, orrery, sextant, lattice, cairn, vellum) all had registered .com plus npm, PyPI and crates.io packages, so they were dropped. Coined names were kept.

## 2. Finalists and availability

## 4. Ranked top 5 (my reasoning)
1. Ovanero: only name with every TLD, GitHub, npm, PyPI, crates free and no same-name product found.
2. Tovinel: same availability; Tovin (a different word) exists as an app.
3. Glenmor: real-sounding, no software conflict; .com taken but .app, .dev, .io free; spelling confusion with Glenmore; Scottish feel.
4. Cendal: .app, .dev, .net free; sounds like Indonesian sendal and Portuguese cendal (gauze); possible TM application.
5. Varnel: .app/.dev free; a Malta consultancy already uses varnel.tech; weak on .com.

Recommendation: Ovanero. Runner-up: Tovinel.
- Neutral: coined, no software, browser or automation meaning. The "-ero" ending reads as a person who does something (es, pt, it).
- Reads: en o-VA-ne-ro; vi easy (o-va-ne-ro, no tone marks needed); es/pt fine, no known word; id fine; th, zh, ja, ru need a transliteration, no known bad meaning; de/fr no known meaning. Not verified by native speakers.
- Domain: ovanero.com free-likely (RDAP 404, no DNS); also .app, .dev, .io, .net free-likely. Take .com first.
- Tagline: "Many profiles, one steady workspace."
- Executable: ovanero.exe (folder Ovanero).
- Risks: 7 letters, 4 syllables, long to type; may be misspelled Ovenaro or Avanero; unknown trademark classes 9 and 42; no brand pull yet.
Rejected: Ostrel (Windows desktop app of the same name), Brimlo (software for freelancers, class 42), Sendal (software company sendal.io; plain sandal in Indonesian).
